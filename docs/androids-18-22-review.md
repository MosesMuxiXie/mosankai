# Chapters 18–22 — Deep Review

Open `index.html` in Edge, Chrome or Firefox. The review works offline without installation, an account or a server. Original-text and classroom-source hyperlinks require internet access.

Start with Before Chapter 18 → Storyline → Detail recall → Character close reading → Themes & synthesis → Original evidence → Mind map → Answer templates → Self-test → Predicted questions. The introduction explains the necessary earlier events, characters and terms. Chapter 18 is reviewed in full; the following chapters cover the ending of the novel.

There are 75 detail cards, 20 extended character readings, 60 multiple-choice questions and 36 short-answer questions. Every chapter owns 12 MC questions. Chapters 18–21 own 7 short answers each; Chapter 22 owns 8. MC categories: 42 detail, 6 concept and 12 analysis. Short-answer categories: 6 detail, 6 concept and 24 analysis. Analytical short answers balance behavior, tone and thought (8 each), with 12 paraphrased scene cues and 12 recall prompts. Combined questions appear in every applicable chapter filter, so filtered totals can overlap.

Each analytical short answer has an exam-length model, detailed reasoning, required points, a common mistake and an acceptable alternative. They are assessed with a checklist, not automatically graded. MC scores remain separate. Predictions are reasoned study priorities, not verified future questions.

Practice Mode reveals feedback after a response is checked. Test Mode reveals models and detailed analysis only after submission of the selected set. Submit again to confirm blanks. Submitted responses are locked until a new attempt. Editing a response in Practice Mode invalidates its old review; editing a short answer clears its checklist. Retry incorrect MC repeats only previously assessed incorrect choices, excluding unanswered questions. New attempt clears only the selected set.

Responses persist across navigation and, where browser storage permits, reloads in the same browser. File and hosted versions have separate browser origins and do not share records automatically. The hosted review is registered as `literature-androids-18-22` and adds favorites, review lists, backups and resume. Its records are independent of the 10–12 and 13–18 reviews, including their overlapping Chapter 18 material. Ordinary offline reloads reset mode and filters while preserving responses.

The story flow starts paused. Use chapter buttons, Previous/Next, scene numbers, full sequence and playback speed. Playback has no audio, stops when leaving the story or hiding the page, and respects reduced-motion settings. The arrows follow reading order; they do not establish exact simultaneity. Use keyboard controls normally; visible focus and responsive layouts support desktop and narrow screens.

Sources use chapter, scene and PDF viewer page numbers from the retained 159-page classroom text. Chapter 18 starts on page 132; Chapter 19 on 141; Chapter 20 on 146; Chapter 21 on 148; Chapter 22 on 153 and ends on 159. Some chapters start partway down a page. All longer scene cues are marked as paraphrases. Six very short verified original fragments appear only in Original evidence.

Important limits: the spider-leg count is inconsistent in the source, so number questions identify the explicit counting moment. Isidore’s vision does not establish the spider’s physical origin. The goat’s killing is reported by Iran; Rick supplies recognition and an inferred motive. The electrical panel settles the toad’s construction, while Mercer’s external status and the cause of its placement remain unresolved. Roy’s grief is considered alongside, not in place of, his treatment of the spider.

Run the content and logic check with Node.js:

```powershell
node .\self-check.mjs
```

The check covers content balance, source references, question feedback, focus filters, scoring, blanks, visibility, state validation and JavaScript syntax. Source checking, visual inspection and browser interaction are separate checks.
