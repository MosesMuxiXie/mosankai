# Study center artwork

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-11. The approved UI concept is a reference for live HTML/CSS, not a flattened page asset. Four independently generated PNGs were inspected, copied into this project as WebP, and resized to 1440px for the hero / 720px for card art (quality 84). Original generated PNGs remain in the Codex image library.

Final files: `public/studyguides/assets/manga-study-room.webp`, `manga-statistics.webp`, `manga-neuroscience.webp`, `manga-english.webp`.

## Shared prompt

Japanese anime background art and colored manga ink, mature minimal editorial illustration, warm ivory paper, fine dark-indigo outlines, soft cel-shaded dusty blue and tiny amber/peach dusk accents, subtle screentone shadows. No text, letters, typography, logo, watermark, UI controls, people or mascot. Wide landscape 16:9, one standalone website illustration, clean crop-safe composition.

## Subject prompts (appended to the shared prompt)

- **Study room:** A THREE-PANEL manga composition with fine ivory gutters and dark ink borders: dominant left two-thirds panel is an empty classroom/library study desk beside a window looking over Japanese urban rooftops at blue dusk with peach sunset; narrow right third consists of upper close-up of warm desk lamp and lower close-up of open unmarked notebook. The illustration ends at its outer rectangle. No website, no screenshot.
- **Statistics:** Focused scholarly still life: unmarked graph paper notebook beside a window with one mathematically accurate symmetric bell-shaped curve centered on a horizontal baseline, delicate under-curve shading and no labels or numerical marks. Few neat pencils. Strong minimal scientific ink drawing with a quiet anime study-room background.
- **Neuroscience:** Scientific educational ink diagram on paper: one neuron, soma at left with branching dendrites, one continuous axon extends toward the right with separated segmented myelin sleeves and terminal branches. Correctly connected, restrained pale-blue shading, minimal paper background, no arbitrary disconnected structures or extra decorative nerves.
- **English:** An open book with abstract tiny ink marks rather than readable writing, beside a classroom window with dusk rooftops, a neat stack of books at left and a few leaves silhouetted at edge. Colored manga panel, quiet literary study corner, clean focused subject.

The thumbnail curve and neuron are conceptual illustration, not the interactive scientific plots or question-bank diagrams. The original review calculations, instructional diagrams and content remain intact.
