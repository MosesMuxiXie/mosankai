04 / Original evidence

The words and what they support

Short original fragments are followed by their speaker, situation, close reading, and limits. Chapter references work across editions; PDF page links locate the exact fragment in the linked English text.

Using a passage in a short answer

Identify the speaker and situation. Select a precise detail, then explain how its wording or placement supports your claim. Connect that explanation to the prompt. A longer quotation does not replace commentary; a paraphrased event can also be valid evidence.
